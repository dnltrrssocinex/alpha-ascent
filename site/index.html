Your account deserves a careful plan

When you have a smaller account, each trade can feel like a big choice. It makes sense to want clear answers before you put money in.

Look at the share cost, the number of shares, and the possible loss. Check how the trade fits your own plan and your broker's account rules.

If the size or risk does not fit, you can wait. You do not have to take every idea.

Build the habit of looking back

Even a well-planned trade can lose money. A review helps you look at the choices you made.

  • What did I plan to do?
  • What did I do when the price moved?
  • What helped me follow my plan?
  • What would I change next time?

You do not have to fix everything at once. Pick one clear habit to practice, then review it again.

Give yourself credit for the habits you build, including the choice to wait.
